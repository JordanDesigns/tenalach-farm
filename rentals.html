Farm Stays

Escape to the countryside. Wake up on a working farm near the Bay of Fundy, surrounded by fields, fresh air, and the peace of rural Nova Scotia.

FEATURED CABIN

Breezy Hill Bunkie

Experience the tranquil charm of our private 160 sq ft bunkie just outside of the Town of Digby. Sitting on the edge of a forest, you can enjoy gentle breezes and sea scents on your private deck as you watch mesmerizing tides dance below. A separate patio with bbq for your meals, a full adjacent bathroom, and a place to simply relax and rest. Minutes from amenities, yet a world apart.
