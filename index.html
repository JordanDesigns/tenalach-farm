Welcome to Tenalach Farm

"To Hear the Earth Sing"

"Tenalach" is an ancient Irish word describing a deep, peaceful connection to the land. The feeling of the earth beneath your feet, the wind through the meadow, and the quiet joy of growing something with your own hands. That spirit lives in everything we do.

Seedlings, Flowers & Herbs

Our seedling benches are bursting with seasonal goodness. From crisp lettuce and tender kale to fragrant herbs and heirloom tomatoes, every plant is grown with sustainable practices and genuine love.

Come Gather at the Farm

Tenalach Farms is more than a place to grow food, it's a place to grow connections. We host workshops, seasonal celebrations, and community events that bring the heart of Digby together.

Whether you're learning to preserve your harvest, joining a wreath-making workshop, or simply enjoying a farm-to-table dinner under the stars, you'll find a warm welcome here.
