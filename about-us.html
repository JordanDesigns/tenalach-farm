Our Story

A Dream Planted in Good Soil

Tenalach Farms began with a simple dream: to live closer to the land and share its gifts with the community. What started as a few raised beds has grown into a thriving farm on the beautiful shores of Digby County.

The name "Tenalach" comes from an old Irish word describing the deep, peaceful connection one feels with the land — that quiet joy of living in rhythm with the seasons.

WHAT WE STAND FOR

Our Values

Sustainability

Organic practices, composting, and mindful growing at the core of everything we do.

MEET THE FARMER

The Heart Behind the Harvest

With dirt under her nails and a vision for something meaningful, Tenalach Farms was born from a passion for growing food, building community, and honoring the land.

What started as a personal garden grew into a mission: to provide fresh, sustainably grown food to the Digby community while creating a space for people to reconnect with nature.

Whether she's tending seedlings at dawn or welcoming guests to the farm stay, every day is guided by the same principle — do it with love, and let the land lead the way.
